# R+2 — Receipts

- **Classification:** PUBLIC-REPRODUCIBLE
- **Publication date of this page:** 2026-08-30

## Purpose

A signed, canonicalised record of what an agent did, chained so that a sequence of actions is
tamper-evident as a sequence and not only individually. Canonicalisation is RFC 8785 (JCS); the base
signature is Ed25519.

## Current status

**An open receipt standard with a normative specification, a conformance corpus dominated by negative
cases, and reference verifiers in several languages, with CI that has demonstrably run and passed. An
operational prototype. Not externally audited, and not production by the programme's own definition.**

## Independent reproduction

A reviewer outside this programme executed the public suite and reproduced **31 of 31 checks in the
verifier run and 31 of 31 in the conformance run**. In browser / WebCrypto mode the same suite reports
**29 of 31**, because the post-quantum leg is **unavailable in that environment and fails closed** —
it refuses rather than degrading silently, which is the intended behaviour and is reported here as a
result rather than omitted as an inconvenience.

*Run date: as published by that reviewer, 2026-08 · publication date of this statement: 2026-08-30.*

**That reproduction is not an audit.** It is one party re-running a public suite and getting the
published answer. It carries exactly the weight that describes.
