## This page is deliberately not written yet

A description of R+4 as a layer does not appear in this portal until **P0-8 closes** — that is, until
the public R+4 repository carries its historical banner and its defect erratum. Publishing a portal
description of R+4 while the repository it points at is unbannered would send readers to a reference
implementation without the warning that belongs on it.

**Leaving this page empty is the correct state today, not an omission.**

## What can be said now, because it is a warning rather than a claim

The public R+4 repository ships the **original** May 2026 circuit. **Defects were found in that circuit
and repaired internally in August 2026.** The repaired circuit is **not published**, is **not frozen**,
and **has no trusted setup of any kind**.

> **Do not rely on the published circuit or the deployed verifier for production assurance.**

The erratum names the **class** of defect and nothing more. **No exploit detail and no proof artefact
demonstrating one is published, here or anywhere, and none will be while external review is
outstanding.**
