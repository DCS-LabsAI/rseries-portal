- **Classification:** PUBLIC-CHECKABLE (the anchor) · PUBLIC-REPRODUCIBLE (the reference code) ·

## Purpose

Export a period's worth of R+2 receipts as a tamper-evident bundle: a Merkle root over the receipts,
signed, and committed to a public chain so that the bundle cannot be silently revised afterwards.

suite, and the first mainnet-anchored bundle — May 2026, five receipts.**

## The gap, stated rather than glossed

**The five receipts behind that root are not yet published.** The root is therefore **accepted as
stated, not recomputed**. A reader can verify that *a* root was committed on-chain at that block; a
reader cannot yet verify that the root is the root of those five receipts, because the receipts are not
available to hash.

Classification of the root's contents: **UNPROVEN**. This is the single largest reproducibility gap in
the best public artefact the programme has, and closing it takes one publication.

## A defect in the public reference, disclosed rather than left to be discovered

The R+3 reference verifier's Merkle check currently **reports a pass in a case where it cannot actually
run** — specifically, when the bundle carries no receipts array to check against, which is the case for
every currently published bundle. **Do not rely on that check's verdict.** It should report *skipped*,
and correcting it is an open item.

This is disclosed here because a control that reports success when it did nothing is the recurring
defect this programme has been correcting across every layer, and hiding an instance of it in the
portal would be the same mistake again.

## Issuer identity

Receipt issuer identity binds to `0xbDd1f5fC349D9a8EfCEb07Edbd491233b2540f5F` on Base. **That is the
contract every published R+3 bundle references.** Its source is **not yet verified on the block
explorer**, and verifying it is an open item. A separate identity contract deployment exists and is
**not** referenced by any published receipt; this portal does not cite it as if it were.
