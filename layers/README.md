## There are no per-layer nodes for R+6 onward

Deliberately. A layer gets its own page when there is public evidence to put on it. Until then it
appears in the R+5 → R+12 status document with its five fields and nothing more, which is the honest
amount of surface for a layer whose source is private.

**R+11 and R+12 in particular have no layer node here**, and will not until their packages are
published. Their internal results are recorded in the status document, labelled as internal results.
