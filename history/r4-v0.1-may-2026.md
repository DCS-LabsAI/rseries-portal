## Part 2 — Errata, appended 2026-08-30

**These are additions. Nothing in Part 1 was changed.** Each erratum states what is corrected, what is
unchanged, and what remains unproven.

### E-1 · Beacon timestamp rendering — UTC

The transcript records the beacon block as *"mined 2026-05-23 00:15:21"* with **no timezone label**.

Bitcoin block **950552** carries `timestamp: 1779475521`, which is **2026-05-22T18:45:21Z**. The
transcript's rendering is the same instant expressed in **UTC+05:30**, unlabelled.

**Unchanged:** the instant, the block height, and the block hash — all three are exact and
independently checkable at the mempool link in Part 1. **Corrected:** only the rendering. In UTC the
calendar date of the beacon block is **22 May 2026**, not 23 May.

*Classification: PUBLIC-CHECKABLE. Run date 2026-05-22/23 · erratum publication date 2026-08-30.*

### E-2 · Contributor independence — against the programme's own roster requirement

The transcript discloses the independence shortfall itself, in Part 1, under *"Honest scope"*. This
erratum adds the standard it is being measured against.

The R+4 coordination plan for this layer requires five contributors **with non-overlapping interests
and infrastructure**, including contributors not employed by DCS Labs, in a different organisation and
country, and recruited through a public call. **The executed ceremony did not meet that roster, and no
per-contributor attestations were published.**

A broader ceremony with **fully independent external contributors** is the required follow-up.

*Classification: PUBLIC-CHECKABLE against the public coordination plan in the same repository.*

### E-3 · The setup-verification line cannot currently be checked by anyone

Part 1 records `snarkjs zkey verify` → `ZKey Ok!`. **That is a written statement of a result.** No
captured output was preserved, and the `.zkey`, `.ptau` and `.r1cs` artefacts needed to re-derive it
are not published.

**Until those artefacts are published, this line cannot be independently checked by anyone.** This
portal does not present it as verified.

*Classification: UNPROVEN — evidence not located. This is not a statement that the command was not
run.*

### E-4 · The contribution window

The R+4 coordination plan specifies a contribution window of **at least fourteen days**. No dated
per-contributor attestations were published, and **the public record of this ceremony spans hours, not
weeks**. The window requirement was **not met on the public record**.

Contributions occurring privately before the public record began are not excluded by anything
available here, and this erratum does not assert that they did not occur.

*Classification: UNPROVEN on the fact · PUBLIC-CHECKABLE on the public record's span.*

### E-5 · Beacon selection order

The coordination plan required the beacon height to be **announced before contributions began**, so it
could not be chosen favourably afterwards. The executed ceremony instead **selected the beacon after
all five contributions completed**, and the transcript says so plainly in Part 1.

Two different anti-selection controls exist, and the programme documented one and executed the other.
The executed control — choosing a block that did not exist when the contributions were made — defeats
a contributor grinding against a known beacon. What the public record does **not** establish is that
block 950552 was still in the future at the moment it was selected.

*Classification: UNPROVEN on the ordering · PUBLIC-CHECKABLE on the block and its hash.*

### E-6 · Layer naming

The transcript's title uses the label *"R+4.5"*. **`R+n` is the canonical public layer naming**, and
this ceremony belongs to **R+4**. The historical title is preserved unedited in Part 1; this erratum
records the canonical name.
