## No receipts are published in this portal yet

**Any receipt that ever appears in this section will be synthetic** — generated for illustration, from
test identities, never a record of a real party's real action. That constraint applies permanently and
without exception.

Today the section is empty, because the receipts that matter most are a different set: **the five
receipts behind the R+3 mainnet-anchored bundle**. Publishing those closes the largest reproducibility
gap in the best public artefact the programme has — it lets anyone recompute the anchored Merkle root
instead of accepting it as stated. See [`../layers/r3.md`](../layers/r3.md).

Publishing decorative synthetic samples before publishing those five would be filling the section
rather than closing the gap.

## No receipt-volume figure is published

Receipt counts have been quoted elsewhere as build-time snapshots. **No public receipt index exists**,
so no such figure is independently verifiable, and none is printed here.
