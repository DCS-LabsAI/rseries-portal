## The honest caveat that belongs on this page

The three standard repositories are the strongest evidence in the estate, and they are also
**under-maintained**. Two of the three have had no commit since May 2026. Correcting that is a
commitment this portal records rather than one it claims to have met.

Corrections to those repositories are made **by addition only** — banners, status files, errata. Their
history is not rewritten, and their visibility is not changed.
